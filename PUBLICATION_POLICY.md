# Publication Policy

Purpose:
Define what can and cannot be published from private Nightcoder Designs repositories into this public proof repository.

This repository is public by design.

Its purpose is to demonstrate operational discipline, systems thinking, and sanitized proof of work without exposing private operational details.

---

## Publication Rule

Public artifacts must show the thinking, structure, and outcomes of the work without exposing sensitive implementation details.

Private repositories remain the source of truth.

This repository contains curated proof, not raw operations.

---

## Allowed Public Material

The following may be published when reviewed and sanitized:

- high-level architecture summaries
- sanitized case studies
- operational lessons learned
- public-safe process descriptions
- proof-of-work notes
- documentation structure examples
- product/service execution summaries
- public-facing decision summaries
- generalized reliability or monitoring lessons
- sanitized diagrams or tables

---

## Prohibited Public Material

Do not publish:

- credentials or secrets
- private keys or tokens
- account identifiers
- customer or client data
- private URLs
- raw logs
- private screenshots
- admin dashboard screenshots
- checkout/account dashboard details
- internal automation scripts
- detailed security control layouts
- sensitive infrastructure details
- unreleased product files
- paid product package contents

---

## Sanitization Checklist

Before publishing an artifact, confirm:

- [ ] No secrets or credentials are included.
- [ ] No account identifiers are included.
- [ ] No private customer/client data is included.
- [ ] No private screenshots are included.
- [ ] No raw logs are included.
- [ ] No checkout, payout, or platform-account details are included.
- [ ] Security controls are described at a safe level of abstraction.
- [ ] The artifact demonstrates a lesson, decision, system, or outcome.
- [ ] The artifact can stand alone without requiring private repo context.

If unsure, do not publish.

---

## Public Proof Standard

A strong public proof artifact should answer:

1. What problem or system is being shown?
2. What constraint mattered?
3. What decision or structure was used?
4. What changed as a result?
5. What lesson can be reused?
6. What details were intentionally omitted for safety?

---

## Repository Boundary

This repository is not a public mirror of private operations.

It is not a product delivery channel.

It is not a sales dashboard.

It is not an incident dump.

It is a curated public proof layer.
